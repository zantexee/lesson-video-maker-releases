# Lesson Video Maker

Turn a MIDI file, a MuseScore score and a performance video into a 1920×1080 piano lesson video. The performance video and moving score sit on the left, and the ball metronome, ornaments and falling-notes piano roll sit on the right.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/zantexee/lesson-video-maker-releases/releases/latest)**:

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and newer) | `LessonVideoMaker-x.y.z-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `LessonVideoMaker-x.y.z-x64.dmg` |
| Windows 10/11 | `LessonVideoMaker-Setup-x.y.z.exe` |

You also need [MuseScore](https://musescore.org) 3 or 4 installed to load `.mscz` scores. ffmpeg is included.

## First launch

The app isn't signed yet, so your computer warns you the first time:

- **Mac:** open the app, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Features

- Piano roll video from MIDI, following the MIDI file's tempo
- Moving MuseScore score
- Ball metronome with beat points
- 21 built-in ornaments, plus a 16×16 ornament creator
- Performance video that you can move, zoom and stretch inside its frame
- Export at a slower BPM (for example, a 40 BPM recording exported at 30 BPM)
- Export presets and batch export
- Undo/redo, autosave and recent projects
- Step frame by frame and jump between key points

## Updates

The app checks for new versions on its own. Windows installs the update when you restart the app. On Mac, the app opens this page so you can download the new version.
